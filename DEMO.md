# CivicResolve 3-Minute Live Demo Script

Follow these steps in sequence for a complete 3-minute walkthrough using the three demo accounts.

1. Open the application at `/login` in your web browser.
2. In the quick-fill box, click **Kavitha S. (CITIZEN)** (`citizen1@civicresolve.gov` / `Password@123`).
3. Click **Sign In** to land on the home dashboard.
4. Review the live Coimbatore statistics strip and the 10-day trend chart.
5. Click **Report Issue** in the navigation bar to open the reporting page.
6. Click **Try an example** to populate sample data or click **Speak with assistant** to use voice input.
7. Select **Gandhipuram** on the map to place an accurate GPS coordinate pin.
8. Click **Submit Complaint** to send the report to the Google Gemini AI pipeline.
9. Inspect the AI assessment card showing classified Category, Severity, Department, and Planning Score.
10. Click **View Public Complaint Page** (`/issue/:id`) to review the public audit timeline.
11. Click the **Support** button to verify self-support prevention (displays "You reported this issue").
12. Click **Logout** from the top right navigation bar.
13. On the login screen, click **Ramesh K. (CITIZEN)** (`citizen2@civicresolve.gov` / `Password@123`).
14. Click **Sign In** and navigate to the previously created issue.
15. Click **Support** to add community endorsement and observe the Community Impact Score increase.
16. Click **Logout** to return to the sign-in screen.
17. Click **Municipal Admin Portal** to navigate to `/admin/login`.
18. Click **Admin Demo Credentials** (`admin@civicresolve.gov` / `Admin@Civic2026!`).
19. Click **Continue to 2-Step OTP** and enter demo code `123456`.
20. Click **Verify & Complete** to enter the Municipal Command Center (`/admin`).
21. Filter the Complaints Queue by priority or department and select the newly submitted complaint.
22. Change status to **In Progress**, select an assigned repair crew, and save updates.
23. Switch to **Help & FAQ** (`/help`) in the header to demonstrate search and bilingual English/Tamil toggling.
